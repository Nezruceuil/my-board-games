# my-board-games

my-board-games is a game that contain all of the games that I like or invented.
It contains a lot of cool background.


### Controls:
- Space to scroll down.
- Shift Space to scroll up
- Control/Option Space to only see the background

### Info:
- The Bots only work with Chess and Castle War.
- Your progress is Saved!

#### Castle War rules:

Castle War is a strategic game played on a 9 by 9 grid.

The objective of the Game is to eat the enemy king.

#### Pieces:

The Wall: The Wall cannot be moved, but pieces of the same team can jump over it (except for the paysan). It can only be eaten by a king, a paysan or a soldier.

The Paysan: the Paysan can be moved 1 square on front of him or 1 square on the side. The paysan eat 1 square on any diagonals. The Paysan promote to a Soldier if he get to the second-to-last rank.

The Soldier: The Soldier move like a king in chess.

The King: The King move like a Soldier / like a king in chess.

The Gunner: The Gunner move like a paysan and shoot 3 square on front of him or 3 square on front of him and moved by 1 on one side.

The Knight: The Knight moves 1 square in any direction, like a king. To capture, it jumps: over one adjacent piece (in any of the 8 directions) to land 2 squares away, eating that piece only if it was an enemy. Jumping a friendly piece is allowed but eats nothing. It can also jump over 2 enemy pieces in a row, but only if they're aligned straight in front, behind, left or right of him (not diagonally), landing 3 squares away and eating both.

The Archer: The Archer moves 1 square in front of him or 1 square on the side. He shoots in an L-shape (like a knight in chess: 2 squares in one direction plus 1 to the side), removing an enemy at that range without moving onto its square.
